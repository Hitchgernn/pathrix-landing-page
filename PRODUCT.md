## Platform

## Users

**Landing page visitor (primary):** MAPID WebGIS Competition 2026 judges/evaluators, reading this site during Top 50 curation and the final judging period. Their job is to decide whether Pathrix's concept, spatial reasoning, and execution deserve to advance — the page's copy and CTA target this reader, not an app-downloading end user.

**Underlying product's end user (once built):** primarily pendatang baru ke Yogyakarta — mahasiswa perantau and wisatawan (domestic/international) with no personal vehicle and no mental map of the city, needing multimodal routes (walk → traditional/mass transit → walk) from one free-text prompt. Secondary: pencari kos/investor properti (accessibility scoring near transit), perencana tata kota Pemda (connectivity to government buildings), and UMKM (visibility via AI Agent recommendations). Served through the same thematic layers as the primary persona, no separate dashboard in v1.

## Product Purpose

Pathrix (working title in the PRD: **TOD Navigator**) is a mobile-first WebGIS built on Transit-Oriented Development principles that integrates Yogyakarta's mass transit (TransJogja, KRL, KA Bandara YIA) with traditional transit (andong, becak) as first/last-mile connectors. Its core is an **AI Agent (Spatial Orchestrator)** — not a chatbot — that toggles map layers, reads the user's active viewport, and composes multimodal routes and multi-stop itineraries from one free-form sentence. Success on this landing page means an evaluator understands that mechanism and its evidence within one scroll.

## Positioning

Conventional WebGIS tools expose dozens of layers/menus that overwhelm a newcomer on a phone with limited time. General navigation apps (Google Maps etc.) don't map andong/becak, Yogyakarta's real first/last-mile connectors. Pathrix's mechanism a competitor can't truthfully copy without the same build: an LLM restricted to **tool-calling orchestration only** (`toggle_layer`, `get_data_in_viewport`, `calculate_route`, `plan_multistop`, `calculate_carbon_savings`) — every number (distance, time, fare, carbon) comes from a structured backend function, never generated by the model, so the guided experience stays hallucination-free.

## Operating Context

- Built for **MAPID WebGIS Competition 2026 — Mass Transportation Edition**; team from Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM). Proposal deadline 27 Jul 2026, Top 50 announced 5 Aug 2026, final submission 14 Sep 2026, showcase 18–23 Sep 2026.
- Product platform on GEO MAPID (hosting/layers) with MAPID MAPS as basemap; no GTFS/public API for TransJogja exists, so transit routes/stops are digitized manually from official maps and field validation.
- Required field survey (MAPID Apps survey activity) maps andong/becak pangkalan — location, hours, fare estimate, geotagged photos — around major transit nodes and tourist spots (Malioboro, Kraton, station areas), scheduled Aug 2026.
- Non-functional targets for the eventual product: AI Agent response < 8s per route; initial load < 5s on 4G; stable uptime during judging.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **In scope (v1):** mobile-first dashboard before the detailed map; multimodal + traditional transit layers; AI Agent with auto-layer toggling and viewport-aware query; multi-stop thematic routing with termudah/tercepat/termurah filters; Sustainability Tracker (gamified carbon savings); Properti/UMKM/Urban Planning thematic layers (Should/Could priority).
- **Out of scope (v1):** ticket payment/booking, minute-level real-time transit schedules (headway/frequency estimates used instead), native mobile app (responsive WebGIS only).
- **Open/undecided:** the "Sosial Budaya" dimension (local etiquette or cultural-site layer) is brainstormed but not scoped — treat as unconfirmed, do not build copy or features around it without asking.
- This repository is the **landing page only** — a separate WebGIS build is out of this repo's scope. No `.env` values are resolved yet (`VITE_MAP_URL`, `VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL`, `VITE_CONTACT_ENDPOINT` all read placeholder fallbacks) — ask before shipping a change that resolves these rather than assuming.

## Brand Commitments

- Name is **Pathrix** (the landing page's own brand), independent of the PRD's internal working title "TOD Navigator" — do not rename the wordmark/brand without the user confirming that trade.
- All copy Indonesian; the hero tagline is deliberately the one English string.
- Visual identity fixed by `Pathrix.dc.html` (the design prototype) and `src/styles/tokens.css`: exactly two page background colors (`--sky-*` light, `--ink*` dark) — never a third. `--on-dark-meta`, `--on-light-meta`, `--warm-text` intentionally deviate from the prototype for WCAG AA contrast.

## Product Principles

1. **Complexity hides behind conversation.** The pitch is that one free-text prompt replaces dozens of GIS layers/menus — every section should reinforce that the AI Agent, not the user, operates the map.
2. **Numbers are computed, never invented.** Mirrors the product's own guardrail (Section 7.5 of the PRD): the landing page must not claim savings, accuracy, or usage figures the product can't back with a real function or citation.
3. **First/last mile is the differentiator.** Andong/becak-as-connector is the one claim a generic navigation app can't make — keep it visible, don't let it get buried under the mass-transit layers.
4. **Judges are the reader, not riders.** Persuade an evaluator that the spatial reasoning and execution are real and rigorous; this is not consumer app marketing copy.
5. **Pre-build honesty.** Nothing on the page should imply a shipped, live product — screenshots are explicitly placeholder, CTA is explicitly provisional.
